Spatial Stillness

Space to
Slow Down.

Solara is a modern sanctuary carved into the dry ochre landscape of the Aravallis. Crafted from local yellow sandstone, unpolished marble, and filtered courtyard shade, it was designed for travelers who seek to shed noise and return to elemental rhythms.

Here, the luxury is not excess, but unmeasured time: the gentle murmur of central stone stepwells, the cooling fragrance of indigenous vetiver, and uninterrupted sightlines toward mountain horizons.

The Environs

Go Beyond
the Resort

While Solara offers sanctuary, the vibrant cultural core of Jaipur lies just thirty minutes down the valley. Our guest concierge arranges low-impact, deeply personal journeys through the city's living history.

01

Amber Fort Dawn Walk

Private sunrise access through the royal Sheesh Mahal mirrors before standard operating hours.

02

City Palace Archives

Private viewings of historic astronomical maps and centuries-old royal miniature art collections.

03

Hawa Mahal Rooftop Dusk

Private rooftop tea tasting overlooking the iconic pink facade during the amber hour.

04

Textile & Gem Guild Walks

Private consultations with traditional sapphire cutters and Dabu mud-resist dye houses.
