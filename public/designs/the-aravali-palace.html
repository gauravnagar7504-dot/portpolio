Our Royal Sanctuary

Where Rajasthan
Feels Like Home.

Perched gracefully amidst the amber crags of the Aravali range, our palace captures the quiet nobility of Rajasthan's golden age. Conceived not merely as an escape, but as a living canvas of traditional scalloped arches, hand-burnished lime plaster, and serene marble courtyards, each corner invites sovereign stillness.

Here, centuries-old Rajput craftsmanship harmonizes with discreet modern indulgence. From the soft flutter of courtyards birds at dawn to bespoke evening gatherings beside our illuminated jal-kund, we curate an intimate hospitality where every guest is welcomed as an honored royal guest of the house.
