DR. AARAV MEHTA Consultant Dentist & Implantologist

“Treatment should begin with understanding, not rushing.”

With over 15 years dedicated to complex restorative dentistry and surgical implantology, Dr. Mehta practices with a quiet, deliberate philosophy: that patient trust is earned through complete diagnostic transparency and conservative tooth preservation.

Following his postgraduate training in Oral Prosthodontics, he completed advanced surgical fellowships in computer-guided implant placement and micro-endodontics. He views each consultation as a collaborative conversation, ensuring patients fully comprehend their clinical roadmaps before any procedure begins.

Before your visit

Clear answers regarding our examination process, implant timelines, and clinical fees.

Please bring any recent dental X-rays or CBCT scans taken within the past 12 months, a list of ongoing medications (especially anticoagulants or osteoporosis medication), and details of relevant medical history. If you do not have previous records, our operatory is equipped to capture digital scans immediately.
We reserve 40 to 50 minutes for all initial evaluations. Dr. Mehta dedicates this time to clinical assessment, reviewing 3D imagery, answering every question, and presenting viable options so you never feel rushed.
